NLP for endangered and underrepresented languages
------

I am an Assistant Professor (*maître de conférences*) at CentraleSupélec ([MICS laboratory](https://mics.centralesupelec.fr/)). 
My research focuses on developing Natural Language Processing (NLP) models for endangered and underrepresented languages. 

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in Natural Language Processing working with [Alexander Fraser](https://alexfraser.github.io/) at the Technische Universität München (TUM) Campus Heilbronn in Germany.
My main work there was on parallel sentence mining for low-resource languages.  
Before that, I was a PhD student at the Université Paris-Saclay (LISN/CNRS laboratory, formerly known as LIMSI) under the supervision of [François Yvon](https://fyvo.github.io/).
My thesis topic was Computational Language Documentation.

My research interests revolve around building models and tools for endangered and underrepresented languages by leveraging available resources.

Research topic keywords: computational language documentation, word and morpheme segmentation, interlinear glossing, parallel sentence mining, multilingual representation, machine translation, NLP for endangered languages, low-resource NLP.
